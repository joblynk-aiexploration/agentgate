# Customer Discovery Questions

Use these questions to learn whether AgentGate solves a painful enough problem and which workflow should become the first focused wedge.

## AI Automation Agencies

1. What kinds of agents are you building for clients today?
2. Which tools do those agents need to touch?
3. What actions feel too risky to let the agent perform automatically?
4. Do clients ask for human approvals before agents act?
5. Do clients ask for audit logs or evidence trails?
6. How do you currently prevent an agent from doing the wrong thing?
7. Would you integrate an API gateway or tool proxy if it helped you sell safer automation?
8. Who would pay for the control layer: your agency or your client?
9. What would make AgentGate easy to include in a client proposal?
10. What integrations would make this immediately useful?

## SaaS Founders

1. Are you adding AI agents to your product?
2. What actions do you want agents to take on behalf of users?
3. Which actions would create customer trust, compliance, or support risk?
4. Do you need per-customer policies for agent behavior?
5. Do your customers ask who approved an AI-driven action?
6. Would an approval inbox help you ship agent features faster?
7. Would you prefer API mode, tool proxy mode, or SDK integration?
8. How much engineering time would you spend building this internally?
9. Would this be a platform feature, security feature, or paid add-on?
10. What would a successful pilot look like?

## Support Automation Teams

1. What support actions are already automated?
2. Which support actions are being considered for AI agents?
3. What refund, credit, cancellation, or account-change thresholds need approval?
4. Who should review high-risk support actions?
5. How do you audit support actions today?
6. What information does a reviewer need before approving a refund or customer message?
7. What happens when automation makes a wrong decision?
8. Do you need an emergency pause button for a bad automation run?
9. How quickly would a blocked-action alert need to reach your team?
10. What support tools would AgentGate need to integrate with first?

## Fintech, Legal, And Healthcare AI Builders

1. What are the highest-risk actions your agents might take?
2. What data types are most sensitive in your workflows?
3. Which actions must always be reviewed by a human?
4. Which actions must be blocked outright?
5. What audit fields are required for compliance or internal review?
6. Do you need role-based approval rules?
7. Do you need separate policies for production, sandbox, and internal environments?
8. How do you prove that an AI system did not bypass controls?
9. What would your security or compliance team need before approving a pilot?
10. Would a local-rules approach be preferred before any AI-based reviewer is introduced?

## Cross-Segment Buying Questions

1. How painful is this problem today on a scale of 1 to 10?
2. What happens if this problem is not solved?
3. Who owns the budget for solving it?
4. What tools would AgentGate need to connect to first?
5. Would you pay for this as a developer tool, security tool, compliance tool, or automation platform feature?
6. What pricing model would feel natural: per organization, per agent, per action, or tiered SaaS?
7. What would block adoption?
8. What would make this urgent?
9. Who else should be in the buying conversation?
10. Can we review one real workflow and map where AgentGate would sit?
